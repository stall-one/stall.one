Case study · business narrative

Problem

Greenfield stacks and plant-floor integrations both suffer from snowflake setup. Teams reinvent Docker compose, env files, and service wiring every time.

Approach

  • CLI-first scaffolding with opinionated defaults and escape hatches.
  • Snap-on module model: add/remove services without rewriting the spine.
  • Document the API surface as the product — install, configure, extend.
Outcome / ROI framing

A developer-facing system for provisioning modular environments with plant-floor and web microservice DNA.

What shipped

Snap-on systems thesis and architecture packaging for portfolio; docs-oriented hub.
