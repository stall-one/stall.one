Case study · business narrative

Problem

Tax and compliance teams drown in unstructured PDFs. Manual classification is slow, error-prone, and hard to audit. Multi-tenant products must isolate data hard — one wrong join is an incident, not a feature request.

Approach

  • Distributed storage with JuiceFS for large document blobs and predictable IO.
  • Tenant-scoped metadata and vectors on Turso/SQLite for lightweight, portable tenancy.
  • Docling + NVIDIA OpenShell for ingestion, prep, and model-backed classification.
  • RAG retrieval gated by tenant policy so answers never cross customer boundaries.
  • Desktop pairing path for firm machines that need local vault access without emailing PDFs.
Outcome / ROI framing

Production SaaS path for sensitive documents: automated classification, searchable knowledge, and architecture that treats isolation as a first-class constraint — not a bolt-on filter.

What shipped

End-to-end pipeline from upload → parse → embed → query, with operational hooks for monitoring and human review when the model is unsure.
