Case study · business narrative

Problem

Industrial and CNC-facing brands often sit between two weak defaults: outdated print catalogs that never make it online, or generic SaaS interfaces that do not feel plant- or shop-real. Buyers need clear stock, cut, and order paths — with a brand that reads premium and transactional.

Approach

  • Lock a print-industrial system first: paper, ink, vermilion, stratified type.
  • Prove the brand in catalog and craft demos (metal stock, forge/cutter) before claiming a full platform product.
  • Shape the stack for commerce: products, stock, cart, orders, and shopper payment — scaffolded with SnapNGo.
  • Keep portfolio packages independent of a live launch; document steps so the brand story is reproducible.
Outcome / ROI framing

A coherent industrial brand kit: design system, shopper-facing catalog journey, interactive craft proof, and a commerce architecture ready for real orders — without shipping a finished market product prematurely.

What shipped

Brand steps note, design post, architecture for SnapNGo + commerce order/billing, CNC metal surface notes, and this hub. Stack is scaffold-ready; focus of the portfolio work is design choices and brand proof.
