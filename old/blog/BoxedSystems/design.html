Why not soft SaaS green

Consumer plant apps use pastel leaves and rounded wellness chrome. Whitebox is industrial CEA: dark shell, high-contrast readouts, green reserved for “in range / healthy.” Operators scan for anomalies in seconds.

Aesthetic pivot

We rejected neon cyber-farm aesthetics. Psychological goal: calm vigilance — the same posture as a good cockpit. Green means go/healthy; amber and red only for out-of-band.

Module cards as UI atoms

Each bay is a card: status, last sensor sweep, next action. Stacking modules in UI mirrors stacking in the rack.

Personas / workflow

Grow tech

Daily checks, nutrient mixes, harvest timing.

Facility lead

Fleet health, energy, expansion planning.

R&D

New module SOPs (myco, HOB) without breaking shell.

Product maturity signal

Design choices here are deliberate pivots with psychological and workflow reasoning — not whatever looked cool that day.
