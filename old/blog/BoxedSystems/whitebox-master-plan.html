LOG_ID: 2026-07 · WHITEBOX · MASTER PLAN

Whitebox bio-industrial platform

Autonomous · Modular · Sustainable. A network of plug-and-play facilities for cultivating biological resources, extracting materials, and processing high-quality products — including alternative proteins — with precision and minimal environmental impact.

ModularAutonomyClosed-loopAlt-protein

01 · Vision

Revolutionize sustainable production via autonomous modular facilities for diverse biological outputs, materials, and specialty chemicals — efficient, precise, low-impact.

02 · Mission

Enable localized, on-demand, resource-efficient production of food, alt-proteins, materials, and chemicals by integrating robotics, AI environmental control, and biology in a scalable Whitebox ecosystem.

03 · Core principles

  • Sustainability — closed-loop, renewables, CO₂ valorization, waste-to-value
  • Modularity — plug-and-play modules for rapid deploy and expansion
  • Autonomy — AI, robotics, analytics for self-optimizing ops
  • Innovation — continuous strain and process R&D
  • Equity — licensing and partnerships to democratize access

04 · Value

  • Lower transport emissions & land use
  • Microbial CO₂-to-protein pathways
  • Lower capex/opex via modular automation
  • Diverse outputs: produce, cultivated meat, biomaterials
  • Distributed resilience & sterility control

05 · Module stack

Phase 1 hydroponics prototype → climate, sensing, PAW, fungi, materials (mycelium), cold-chain modules, microbial protein — compose facilities like software packages.

See: hydro BOM, mycelium packaging, HOB protein.

Thesis

Localize production. Modularize biology. Automate the climate and the loop.

Takeaway

Whitebox is the umbrella: stack modules until a site feeds, packages, and processes with closed loops.
