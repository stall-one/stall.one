LOG_ID: 2026-07-25 · WHITEBOX · HYDROPONICS · BOM

Whitebox hydroponics module prototype

Phase 1 proof of concept for a modular bio-industrial platform: a compact 1000 × 750 × 2000 mm unit with hybrid climate control, Raspberry Pi 5 intelligence, and multi-tier NFT for leafy greens in urban spaces.

NFTPeltier + HVACRPi 5$750–1100

01 · Why it matters

  • Modular & stackable — plug-and-play scaling
  • Smart — hybrid Peltier + micro-HVAC + Pi 5 with room for ML
  • Sustainable targets — high water recycling, efficient climate
  • Phase 1 goal — validate core tech before fungi / alt-protein modules

02 · System snapshot

4–6 tiers of leafy greens, herbs, and roots via NFT/aeroponic methods. Target climate roughly 20–25°C and 60–80% RH. UV-C for sterilization. Sensors for pH/EC and enclosure conditions.

04 · Assembly path

  1. Frame — T-slot base, 2 m verticals, tier supports ~every 300 mm
  2. Enclosure — insulated reflective panels; seal for humidity control
  3. Grow — NFT channels, net pots, reservoir recirculation
  4. Climate / light — LEDs per tier, Peltiers, micro-HVAC, UV-C
  5. Electronics — sensors → Pi 5; automation scripts
  6. Test — dry/wet, calibrate, leak check

05 · Pro tips & next

3D-print mounts, label wires, design for future stacking and central multi-module control. Next modules: fungi, robotics hooks, microbial protein and cultivated-meat adjacent systems.

Design bias

Ship a vertical farm as a BOM, not a brochure. Validate climate and recirculation before stacking buildings.

Takeaway

Full instructions and costing live in this log for Phase 1. Pair with PAW fertigation experiments once the reservoir is stable.
