[PLATFORM: WHITEBOX BIO]

Autonomous modular bio-industrial facilities — stackable hydroponics, multi-modal crop sensing, plasma fertigation hooks, and a roadmap toward microbial protein and materials.

Role
Systems architect
Stack
RPi · NFT · RF/mmWave · PAW
Domain
CEA / Bio-industry
Status
Prototype + docs
MODULAR HYDROPONICS SENSING CLOSED_LOOP

Problem

Centralized food and materials supply chains are brittle, land-hungry, and slow to adapt. Urban and peri-urban sites need production units that are plug-and-play, sensor-rich, and expandable — not one-off greenhouses.

Approach

  • Define a Whitebox master plan: modular, autonomous, sustainable facilities as a network.
  • Ship Phase 1 hydroponics module (1×0.75×2m NFT) with hybrid climate control and Raspberry Pi brain.
  • Layer multi-modal sensing (probes + mmWave/ISAC path + shared high-fi scanner) for health, pests, and harvest timing.
  • Integrate sustainable inputs (e.g. plasma-activated water as partial N + sanitizer) and materials R&D (mycelium packaging/leather).
Outcome

A coherent platform story: local production modules that can expand into protein, fungi, and materials without rewriting the facility shell.

What shipped / built

Master plan documentation, hydro BOM and assembly path, RF sensing architecture report, links into mycelium and HOB protein research tracks.
