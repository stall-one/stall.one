Plasma Activated Water for hydroponics

Water treated with cold (non-thermal) plasma is infused with reactive oxygen and nitrogen species (RONS) — nitrates, nitrites, H₂O₂, and more. It mimics lightning fixing nitrogen in rain. In hydro systems it can act as partial fertilizer, biostimulant, and sanitizer using only air, water, and electricity.

RONSNitrateSanitizerCEA

01 · What PAW is

Plasma Activated Water is produced by exposing water to plasma from electrical discharge in air. RONS dissolve into the bulk liquid. Methods include DBD, plasma jets, and pinhole discharges — batch reactors or continuous inline flow for fertigation lines.

02 · Hydro benefits

  • Nitrogen & growth — bioavailable N (mainly nitrate); lettuce studies show stronger radicles, chlorophyll, foliar area, dry weight; can cut synthetic N dependence.
  • Germination — better rates and seedling vigor for sprouts and starts.
  • Sanitation — suppresses bacteria, fungi (e.g. Pythium), biofilms, algae; species decay, no persistent toxic residue.
  • Stress & quality — resilience gains; sometimes better nutrition profiles / lower leaf nitrate vs conventional N.
  • Sustainability — less synthetic fertilizer; fits recirculating systems (watch EC/pH).

03 · Production & dosing

Dilution is everything. Typical use: low % of reservoir volume, or target nitrate bands (e.g. ~1.5–3 mg/L NO₃⁻ in dilute forms; higher when concentrating). Overly strong PAW can crash pH and stress roots.

Commercial options are emerging (e.g. VitalFluid-class systems for greenhouses). DIY HV setups exist — high voltage is hazardous; uncontrolled designs can make bad byproducts or do nothing useful.

04 · Practical tips

  • Apply as fertigation, seed soak, or reservoir top-up; inline reactors before the tank work well.
  • Monitor pH (often acidic), EC, nitrate, ORP. Species decay — prefer fresh PAW.
  • Start as partial N replacement next to full K/Ca/Mg etc. nutrients.
  • Leafy greens, sprouts, tomatoes, herbs are the common trial crops.

05 · Limits

  • RONS (esp. H₂O₂) are unstable — use soon after generation.
  • Dose optimization required to avoid phytotoxicity.
  • Capex + energy; scale needs engineering.
  • Research is promising, not a complete nutrient solution.
Design bias

Pair PAW with a full hydro recipe. Treat plasma as a nitrogen + sanitation co-pilot, not a magic bottle that replaces chemistry.

Takeaway

Start small, measure, log pH/EC/NO₃⁻/ORP, and expand only after crop-specific trials. Related: Whitebox hydro module.
