Mycelium leather production

A biodegradable alternative to animal and plastic leathers: grow fungal sheets on ag waste in 1–3 weeks, dry, press, and finish. Commercial players (MycoWorks, Bolt Threads / Mylo) industrialize it; DIY uses Reishi, oyster, king trumpet, or shiitake at home scale.

GanodermaPleurotusSheet growthLow water

01 · Why mycelium leather

Custom strength/texture/flexibility, low water/energy vs cattle leather (~17,100 L/kg cited for animal hide), no slaughter, compostable end-of-life. Grown in weeks, not months of animal rearing.

02 · Materials

  • Substrate — hardwood sawdust, wheat straw, coir, chips, paper, cardboard; C:N ~45–60:1; 1–2 kg/small batch
  • Mycelium — liquid culture, grain spawn, or tissue; Reishi for durable sheets; oyster/shiitake for flexible
  • Molds — trays, tupperware, bags; plastic-lined; clear containers help monitoring
  • Sterility kit — boiling water, IPA, optional pressure cooker, gloves, mask

03 · DIY process

  1. Substrate — chop 1–2 cm. Paper/cardboard: boil 20–30 min, drain to 50–75% moisture. Sawdust/coir/straw: steam/pasteurize 60–80°C 1–2 h; optional 20% bran. Cool fully. Paste method: blend thick for even sheets.
  2. Inoculate — alcohol-clean tools. Mix spawn 5–10% w/w (e.g. 100 g/kg). Layer method: alternate corrugated sheets + mycelium for air gaps. Pack 1–2 cm thick; compress for density. Seal (high CO₂ favors thick mycelium, less fruiting); tiny vents only if needed.
  3. Incubate — dark, 20–28°C, 85–95% RH. Brief daily vent for CO₂. White fuzzy spread in 2–7 days; full colonize 5–14 days (to ~3 weeks thick). Low O₂ → foamier aerial mats.
  4. Harvest — firm white mat; unmold/cut sheets carefully; delaminate paste runs gently.
  5. Dry & finish — press (weights/stand) to compact. Oven 80–93°C for 2–24 h to <30% moisture. Optional: natural tan (salt/boil), oil for flex, dye. Heat stops growth; brief higher heat hardens.

04 · Variations

  • Reishi → durable; oyster → faster
  • Coir → thick flexible; paper → moldable but can crack; sawdust → strength
  • Aerial mats: flat trays, lean substrate, controlled O₂

05 · Safety & environment

  • Gloves/mask if spore-sensitive; ventilate
  • Discard contaminated runs (green/black)
  • High CO₂ favors mycelium over mushrooms
  • Waste-based, low water, biodegradable
Design bias

Leather quality is density + finish. Sterility and CO₂ management decide whether you get a usable sheet or a fruiting mess.

Takeaway

Start with coir + oyster or Reishi grain spawn, grow thin sheets, press hard, dry completely. Pair with mycelium packaging — same organism toolkit, different product geometry.
