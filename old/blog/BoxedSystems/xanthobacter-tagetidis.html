LOG_ID: 2026-07-25 · MICROBIOLOGY · HOB · SOLEIN-PROXY

Xanthobacter tagetidis DSM 11105

Yellow-pigmented, Gram-negative, pleomorphic rod (0.4–1.0 × 0.8–6.0 µm; sometimes branched or twisted). Isolated from root balls of Tagetes (marigold) in the UK. A publicly available hydrogen-oxidizing bacterium (HOB) proxy for Solein-adjacent gas-fed protein work.

H₂ + CO₂ + O₂RuBisCO / CBBN₂ fixRG1 / BSL-1~80.4% OrthoANI vs SoF1

01 · Metabolism

Facultatively chemolithoautotrophic. Grows on H₂ + CO₂ + O₂ (hydrogen-oxidizing) using the Calvin–Benson–Bassham cycle via RuBisCO for CO₂ fixation.

Also chemolithotrophic on thiosulfate / inorganic sulfur compounds, and heterotrophic/mixotrophic on substituted thiophenes (e.g. thiophene-2-carboxylic acid, T2C).

02 · Nitrogen & growth

Diazotrophic under microaerobic conditions; can use fixed N (e.g. NH₄⁺). Mesophile (opt. 28–31°C, ~25–37°C range). Neutrophile (opt. pH ~7.6–7.8 on T2C; broader ~6–8 autotrophic).

Obligate aerobe; microaerobic-tolerant for N₂ fixation. Max growth rate ~0.22 h⁻¹ on T2C.

03 · Solein relevance

Closest publicly available relative to Solar Foods’ proprietary Xanthobacter sp. SoF1 (OrthoANI ~80.4%). SoF1 remains preferred for industrial protein, but X. tagetidis shares core HOB traits: H₂ oxidation, CO₂ fixation, yellow biomass.

Expect slower growth than SoF1, with observable yellow turbidity and biomass carrying substantial protein potential (often cited in the 40–60%+ range under good conditions — measure, don’t assume).

04 · Cultivation media (DSMZ-oriented)

Mode Media
Heterotrophic DSMZ Medium 1 (Nutrient Agar) or Medium 428 (H3P: sugars/acids, YE, NH₄Cl, …)
Autotrophic / minimal N-free or low-N mineral salts: phosphates, MgSO₄, FeSO₄, traces (Mo, V, Ni for hydrogenases). No organics for strict autotrophy.
Incubation 30°C · aerobic or microaerobic shake (120–800 rpm)

05 · Biohacking protocol adaptations

// 1. Revival
rehydrate freeze-dried → 5–10 mL Medium 1 or mineral medium
30°C · 48–72 h

// 2. Heterotrophic preculture
Medium 428 or 0.2% organics (succinate, methanol, …)
build biomass → wash → shift autotrophic

// 3. Autotrophic / H₂ growth
electrolysis H₂/O₂ or defined mix
~50–70% H₂ · 15–25% CO₂ · 5–20% O₂
(microaerobic if targeting N₂ fixation)
watch yellow turbidity + dry weight / protein

// 4. Thiophene bonus (unique trait)
test T2C · opt. 28–31°C · pH 7.6–7.8

06 · Acquisition

  • DSMZ: freeze-dried or active (~€120–280)
  • ATCC: similar availability / pricing
  • No Nagoya Protocol restrictions noted for this strain
  • Risk Group 1 — appropriate for BSL-1 labs

07 · Expectations

Excellent accessible proxy for enrichment-style or gas-fed Solein-inspired setups. High yields still need continuous culture, tight gas control, and process engineering — not just strain choice.

Design bias

Start heterotrophic to revive and build biomass, then transition to H₂-based autotrophy. Treat X. tagetidis as a public HOB workhorse, not a drop-in SoF1 clone — same broad physiology, different industrial fitness.

Takeaway

DSM 11105 is a yellow HOB type strain with real CO₂-fixation and N₂-fix tools for open experiments. Order it, revive carefully, validate growth on your gas train, then optimize. Related protein-process notes: clear BiB protein and food pods (different layer of the stack).
