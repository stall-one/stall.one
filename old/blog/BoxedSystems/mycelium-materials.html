[MATERIALS: MYCELIUM]

Home-scale mycelium packaging (foam replacement) and mycelium leather sheets grown on agricultural waste — biodegradable materials for packaging and soft goods.

Role
Materials experimentation
Stack
Pleurotus · Reishi · Ag waste
Domain
Biomaterials
Status
DIY protocols
MYCELIUM PACKAGING LEATHER BIODEGRADABLE

Problem

Plastic foam and animal/synthetic leather carry heavy environmental costs. Accessible DIY paths for fungal materials are fragmented across tutorials.

Approach

  • Packaging: inoculate ag waste, colonize in molds 5–10 days, heat-dry to kill and stiffen.
  • Leather: paste/mat growth at 1–2 cm, high-CO₂ sealed incubation, press + dry + optional oil/tan.
  • Species/substrate matrix: oyster for speed; Reishi for durability; coir/sawdust/paper tradeoffs.
Outcome

Two production playbooks that plug into Whitebox materials modules and circular packaging stories.

What shipped / built

Full packaging and leather DIY guides with safety and contamination notes.
