Multi-modal RF sensing for precision hydroponics

Contactless monitoring of plants, nutrient solutions, ripeness, pests, and environment — mmWave radar, 5G/6G ISAC, hyperspectral imaging, acoustics, ML fusion, RIS, and consumer LiDAR NLOS — for modular stackable hydro systems.

mmWaveISACHSIRISLiDAR NLOS

01 · Why RF

RF senses via reflections and channel perturbations — works in dark, dust, humidity, partial occlusion; privacy-friendly (no camera). Platforms: Wi-Fi CSI, RFID, radar, SDR.

02 · mmWave radar

24/60/77 GHz FMCW for range, velocity, angle at cm resolution: canopy height/biomass, leaf water stress, substrate moisture, micro-Doppler insect activity.

03 · 5G/6G ISAC

Same waveforms for comms + sensing. Central ISAC node can control boxes and sense across a stack, cutting redundant radios. mmWave bands enable high resolution.

04 · Applications

  • Substrate/nutrient moisture & uptake proxies
  • Growth dynamics without contact
  • Ripeness via dielectric/mechanical change
  • Pest signatures (wing-beat, vibration)
  • Closed-loop dosing/light/irrigation with probe fusion

05 · Hybrid architecture

  • Per-box (cheap, always-on): pH/EC/TDS, T/RH/level, optional compact mmWave/Wi-Fi, basic acoustic; ESP32-class + local automation
  • Central scanner (shared, high-fi): mmWave + hyperspectral + acoustic array + optional LiDAR; dock/gantry periodic scans
  • Data layer: edge preprocess + ML fusion → health scores, ripeness, pest alerts, control actions

06 · ML + HSI + acoustic

CSI / range-Doppler / spectrograms → RF/SVM/CNN/LSTM/transformers. HSI for early chlorophyll, water, nutrient, disease signatures (periodic). Acoustic taps validate firmness/ripeness and pest vibration.

07 · RIS & LiDAR NLOS

Intelligent reflecting surfaces extend mmWave into shadowed tiers. Consumer ToF LiDAR research enables seeing around corners into occluded layers — complementary to RF.

08 · Recommendations

Start probes + basic RF/acoustic per box + one shared multi-modal scanner. Progressive labeling, validate vs manual scouting, expand RIS/ISAC/LiDAR as costs fall. Challenges: data volume, calibration, fan noise, occlusion.

Thesis

Low-cost always-on boxes + shared laboratory-grade scanner is the practical path to precision CEA sensing today.
