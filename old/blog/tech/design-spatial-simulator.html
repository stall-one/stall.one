Aesthetic pivot

Rejected busy sci-fi HUDs that steal GPU and attention. Minimal cyan readouts, large hit areas for orbit/pan, scene is the hero.

Interaction

Mouse/touch orbit defaults; advanced physics toggles secondary. Onboarding is a single gesture hint, not a tutorial modal stack.

Personas / workflow

Portfolio visitor

Wants immediate “wow” without install.

Frontend eng

Inspects frame budget and React boundaries.

Designer

Cares about control discoverability.

Product maturity signal

Design choices here are deliberate pivots with psychological and workflow reasoning — not whatever looked cool that day.
