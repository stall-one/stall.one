[PLATFORM: TAXFORT]

Multi-tenant AI document engine for sensitive tax workflows — classification, retrieval, and audit trails without collapsing under scale.

Role
Full-stack + AI
Stack
RAG · Turso · JuiceFS
Domain
Document SaaS
Live
tax.swenusa.com
MULTI_TENANT RAG_PIPELINE NVIDIA_OPENSHELL

Problem

Tax and compliance teams drown in unstructured PDFs. Manual classification is slow, error-prone, and hard to audit. A multi-tenant product also has to isolate data hard — one wrong join and you have an incident, not a feature request.

Approach

  • Distributed storage with JuiceFS for large document blobs and predictable IO.
  • Tenant-scoped metadata and vectors on Turso/SQLite for lightweight, portable tenancy.
  • Docling + NVIDIA OpenShell for ingestion, OCR-adjacent prep, and model-backed classification.
  • RAG retrieval gated by tenant policy so answers never cross customer boundaries.
Outcome

Production SaaS path for sensitive documents: automated classification, searchable knowledge, and an architecture that treats isolation as a first-class constraint — not a bolt-on filter.

What shipped

End-to-end pipeline from upload → parse → embed → query, with operational hooks for monitoring and human review when the model is unsure. Built for operators who need both speed and a paper trail.
