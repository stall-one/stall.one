[SYSTEM: THE ENCLAVE]

Economic enclavism — a high-trust sovereign pocket inside the city: civic contributions instead of rent, shared Sovereign Grid, and Enclave Stable Credit (ESC).

Role
Product + systems design
Stack
Membership · ESC · Grid · Governance
Domain
Community infrastructure
Status
Whitepaper v1.2
ENCLAVISM ESC HIGH_TRUST PARALLEL_BUILD

Problem

Cities extract wealth via rising rent, utility monopolies, and low-trust services. Capital leaks outward; residents get little agency or upside.

Approach

  • Parallel construction inside the city — not escape, not city-hall reform theater.
  • Replace rent with civic contributions that fund shared infrastructure.
  • Sovereign Grid for negotiated/shared utilities; internal marketplace for low-fee trade.
  • ESC: fiat-backed internal credit for seamless local spend/earn/redeem.
  • Character-first membership with Associate → Full → Genesis tiers.
Outcome

A practical thesis for capital retention and high-trust living: fix the footprint, not the entire city. Replicable nodes that can federate.
