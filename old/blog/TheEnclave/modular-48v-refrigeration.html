48V DC modular refrigeration

In a theoretical 48V DC-native parallel system, the compressor and condenser are shared centrally. Each module — fridge, freezer, drink/ice — keeps localized components for independent control and safe operation.

Shared compressorEEV48V busDaisy-chain

01 · Shared vs local

Central: compressor + condenser (expensive, variable-speed friendly).

Per module: evaporator + fan, flow control, sensors, defrost, safety, insulation, door switch. Temperature independence without duplicating the big metal.

02 · Minimum per module

  • Evaporator + 48V fan — sized to load / setpoint
  • Solenoid (48V) — opens only when cooling is needed
  • EEV — preferred over TXV for superheat + variable-speed systems
  • Temp sensors — cabinet air + evaporator surface; local node ↔ central brain
  • Defrost — 48V electric heater or hot-gas; termination sensor; drain heat if needed
  • Safety — pressure switch/transducer; fuses; filter-drier (shared or per line)
  • Structure — high-performance insulation; door switch for logic

03 · Drink / ice extras

Higher evaporator capacity or dedicated ice evaporator; 48V water/circulation pump for flake/nugget styles; more frequent defrost under ice load.

04 · Backplane

  • Piping — liquid in + suction out on daisy-chain lines
  • Power — all loads on central 48V DC bus
  • Comms — modules report temp/status; central sets compressor speed and which solenoids/EEVs fire

Design bias

Takeaway
