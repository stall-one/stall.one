Clear, pumpable protein concentrate for bag-in-box / fountain

Guide and formulation overview: hydrolyzed whey protein isolate (HWPI) and plant-based alternatives — built for soda-fountain style dispensing with a clear, non-milky finish after dilution.

HWPIClear peapH 3.0–4.2UHT / BiB15–25g / serve

01 · Project goals

  • Neutral or lightly flavored liquid concentrate
  • Clear / non-milky when dispensed and diluted (soda / sparkling-water look)
  • Pumpable through standard bag-in-box fountain systems
  • High protein: 15–25g per serving after dilution
  • Shelf-stable or extended refrigerated life; flavor-customizable

02 · Protein choices

Primary — HWPI: excellent solubility and clarity in acid, fast-absorbing, low lactose/fat, solid heat stability for process.

Plant alternative — hydrolyzed pea (e.g. clear grades): vegan path; specialized “clear” products for transparency and low viscosity. Slightly harder on stability, still viable with care.

03 · Clarity & pumpability principles

Lever Target / note
pH 3.0–4.2 (citric, malic, or phosphoric) — clarity + microbial control
Concentration 15–30%+ protein in concentrate; dilute ~1:4 to 1:6 at dispense
Viscosity Keep low; glycerin for flow / mouthfeel without clouding
Additives Stevia / sucralose; K-sorbate + Na-benzoate; optional electrolytes & clear flavors

04 · Basic HWPI concentrate (1 L batch)

200–300 g  HWPI powder
700–900 ml water
           acid → pH 3.5–4.0
50–100 ml  glycerin
           sweetener + preservatives to taste / safety

05 · Processing for stability

Refrigerated: pasteurize (HTST) + preservatives → roughly 1–4 weeks.

Shelf-stable BiB (recommended): UHT 135–150°C (often 140–145°C), hold 2–5 s, aseptic fill into BiB bags — commercial sterility, months ambient. Minimize heat load; prefer direct UHT when possible; pre-filter; re-check clarity after process.

Hot-fill: alternative for acid products (fill hot >180°F, then cool).

06 · Dispensing

Standard soda fountain BiB: pump concentrate, mix with carbonated or still water. Final drink should read clear and refreshing with high protein — pre-flavor batches or build flavors on the fly.

Pairs naturally with high-throughput formats (cafés, gyms, robotic drink lines) where milky RTDs are the wrong aesthetic.

07 · Challenges & fixes

Issue Mitigation
Clarity loss Strict pH, high-purity protein, filtration
UHT fouling / instability Small-batch validation; stabilizers only if proven necessary
Bitter notes (hydrolysates) Flavor + sweetener masking matrix
Scale path Home/lab → pasteurized cold chain; commercial → UHT + aseptic

08 · Next steps

  • Source high-quality HWPI or clear-grade hydrolyzed pea
  • Pilot batches: pH, visual + NTU clarity, pump flow
  • Microbial stability and shelf-life validation
  • Food technologist for scaled UHT + regulatory compliance
Design bias

Treat clarity and pumpability as first-class specs — not flavor afterthoughts. Acidic pH is the lever that makes “clear protein soda” possible; process heat is the lever that makes BiB logistics possible. Validate both before branding the SKU.

Takeaway

HWPI (and clear plant hydrolysates) can power a fountain-style protein base when concentrate strength, acidity, viscosity, and thermal process are designed together. Related product narrative: robotic food pods — different format, same “fuel without friction” idea.
