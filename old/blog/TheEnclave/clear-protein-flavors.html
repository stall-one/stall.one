Flavors for freestyle clear protein

Neutral clear protein base + on-demand modules (caffeine, probiotics, creatine, biotin, collagen, electrolytes) needs bright, acid-stable, fully clear flavors that mask bitterness and mix freely on a touchscreen freestyle UI.

pH 3.2–3.6Clear PROFreestyleModules

01 · Requirements

  • Fruit-forward, refreshing — fits non-dairy clear profile
  • Acid-stable and crystal clear when mixed
  • Masks caffeine/module notes
  • Composable for freestyle combinations

02 · Flavor families

  • Citrus — lemonade, lime, orange, yuzu (best caffeine mask)
  • Berry — strawberry lemonade, mixed berry, raspberry (probiotic-friendly)
  • Tropical — mango passion, light pineapple coconut, guava
  • Herbal — mojito, cucumber mint, ginger lime (collagen/biotin)
  • Stone fruit — peach, watermelon, green apple

03 · Freestyle combos

ModeModulesExample
Workout energyCaffeine + creatinePerformance Citrus Rush

04 · Dev notes

  • Test every combo for haze/sediment
  • Citrus/berry for bitterness; keep coconut light for clarity
  • 4–6 core flavors + user mixes keep SKU count sane
  • Still vs sparkling changes mouthfeel dramatically
  • Sucralose/stevia/monk fruit blends for clear low-pH sweetness

05 · Related stack

Pairs with BiB concentrate formulation and fountain/robotic dispense concepts.

Thesis

Flavor is product differentiation; clarity is non-negotiable. Never ship a cloudy freestyle SKU.

Takeaway

Start with 4–6 acid-stable cores, prove module combos, then open freestyle mixing.
