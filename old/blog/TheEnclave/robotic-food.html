[PRODUCT: ROBOTIC FOOD PODS]

Autonomous hybrid-wok food pods with Bowl Credits subscription economics, backed by clear protein concentrate formulation and freestyle flavor architecture.

Role
Product + process design
Stack
Robotics · BiB · Clear protein · Subscription
Domain
Food tech / retail
Status
Concept + formulation
ROBOTICS SUBSCRIPTION HYBRID_WOK CLEAR_PROTEIN

Problem

Daily fuel is stuck between sad meal prep and expensive fast-casual lines. Restaurant overhead (rent, labor, waste) prices customers out of consistency.

Approach

  • Bypass restaurant real estate: autonomous pods, ~90s cook, premium cubby pickup.
  • Subscription Bowl Credits + Volume Bar (no ingredient upcharges).
  • Hybrid-wok + pressurized air for sears and crisp textures without deep-fry oil vats.
  • Upstream: clear pumpable protein BiB concentrate; freestyle flavor families for modular machines.
Outcome

A full stack from concentrate chemistry to retail UX: fixed monthly economics, robotic consistency, South Florida pilot narrative (Pompano Beach).

What shipped / built

Product narrative post, BiB formulation guide, freestyle flavor matrix.

Technical logs

Food pods · BiB protein · Flavor system
