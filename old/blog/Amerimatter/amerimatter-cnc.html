Problem

Manufacturers need clear catalog commerce, floor-real systems consulting, and premium brand storytelling — but those surfaces demand different visual contracts (paper catalog ≠ void-sky film).

Approach

  • cnc-metal: paper/ink/navy catalog, product plates, Forge 3D cutter, shop guides.
  • Services thesis: snap-on microservices + hardware integration for US plants (assess → design → snap → harden → expand).
  • Cinematic lane: WebGPU depth hero, scroll-scrub film, aerospace-industrial tokens — kept separate from print.
Outcome

Lane-separated design systems so plant catalogs never inherit aerospace voids (or vice versa), with a coherent reindustrialization narrative.

What shipped / built

Self-contained cnc-metal site package, snap-on systems log, cinematic scroll/WebGPU notes.
