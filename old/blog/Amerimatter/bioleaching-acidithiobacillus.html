Acidithiobacillus: thiooxidans vs ferrooxidans

Not all bioleaching bacteria are the same. Acidithiobacillus thiooxidans (sulfur oxidizer) is not the Universal Microbes “Thiobacillus ferrooxidans” product (iron + sulfur oxidizer, now A. ferrooxidans). Both matter for copper/gold from scrap PC e-waste — best as a mixed culture.

Urban miningpH 1.5–4Mixed cultureRG awareness

01 · Taxonomy

Both are acidophilic Acidithiobacillus (reclassified from Thiobacillus ~2000). They thrive at low pH and drive biomining chemistry.

  • A. thiooxidans — primarily sulfur → sulfuric acid; does not oxidize Fe²⁺
  • A. ferrooxidans — Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ (powerful oxidant) and sulfur compounds

Universal Microbes powder marketed as Thiobacillus ferrooxidans matches the iron-oxidizing profile of A. ferrooxidans.

02 · Why it matters for PCBs

  • Ferrooxidans alone → strong indirect leaching via biogenic Fe³⁺
  • Thiooxidans → sustained acid production
  • Mixed 1:1 often wins — high copper recovery and solid gold mobilization in lab literature

Pro tip: start with affordable ferrooxidans powder, then source thiooxidans (ATCC/DSMZ) for a consortium. Revive in 9K medium, 28–30°C, watch pH drop and Eh.

03 · Home lab setup

  • Safety — acid PPE, ventilation
  • Reactor — shaken flasks or DIY column + peristaltic loop
  • Process — two-step: grow bacteria, then 1–10% pulp density e-waste (reduces toxicity)
  • Timeline — growth 7–14 days; leaching weeks

04 · Bottom line

Don’t assume the Universal Microbes product is thiooxidans — it’s the iron-oxidizing workhorse ferrooxidans. Pair both for urban mining experiments that are effective and educational.

Design bias

Complementary chemistries beat brand-name confusion. Acid + Fe³⁺ is the dual engine of scrap PCB bioleaching.

Takeaway

Start ferrooxidans, add thiooxidans, measure pH/Eh, keep pulp density honest. Related microbial logs: Xanthobacter.
