Case study · business narrative

Problem

Hardware/product storytelling fights soft SaaS chrome. Marketing spectacle and production UI also diverge — without a shared contract, every screen invents a new gray.

Approach

  • Hard edges, solid faces, radius zero — specification-sheet aesthetics.
  • Marketing stage: horizontal blinds scroll demo (GSAP/Lenis) for Hydro/Myco/Cryo modules.
  • Living style guide: foundations, layout primitives, components, progressive JS (theme, modal, toast).
  • Class contract: bx- for product, data-bx-* hooks, ds- docs-only chrome.
Outcome / ROI framing

A dual-surface brand: motion where it earns attention, boring progressive components where operators live.

What shipped

Boxed-Systems marketing demo + design-system kit documented in the technical log.
