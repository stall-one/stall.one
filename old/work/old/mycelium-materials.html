Case study · business narrative

Problem

Petroleum foams and animal leather dominate by default. Builders need honest DIY paths with realistic timelines and failure modes.

Approach

  • Oyster mycelium packaging on ag waste (7–14 day grow windows).
  • Reishi/oyster leather sheets: paste, mat growth, dry, finish.
  • Document contamination and drying as first-class failure modes.
Outcome / ROI framing

Two practical material tracks that can plug into Whitebox materials modules later.

What shipped

Packaging and leather production guides with home/pilot framing.
