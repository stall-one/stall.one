Case study · business narrative

Problem

Centralized food and materials supply chains are brittle, land-hungry, and slow to adapt. Urban sites need production units that are plug-and-play, sensor-rich, and expandable — not one-off greenhouses.

Approach

  • Whitebox master plan: modular, autonomous facilities as a network.
  • Phase 1 hydroponics module (1×0.75×2m NFT) with hybrid climate and Raspberry Pi brain.
  • Multi-modal sensing (probes + mmWave/ISAC + shared high-fi scanner) for health, pests, harvest timing.
  • Sustainable inputs (PAW as partial N + sanitizer) and materials R&D tracks (mycelium, HOB protein).
Outcome / ROI framing

A coherent platform story: local production modules that expand into protein, fungi, and materials without rewriting the facility shell.

What shipped

Master plan documentation, hydro BOM and assembly path, RF sensing architecture, links into mycelium and HOB research.
