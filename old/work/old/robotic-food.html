Case study · business narrative

Problem

Performance meals are either expensive chef-ish delivery or sad protein powder. Automation without formulation discipline produces expensive mediocrity.

Approach

  • Hybrid-wok pods for high-throughput assembly.
  • Bowl Credits as subscription unit economics.
  • Clear pumpable protein BiB for fountain/pod dispense.
  • Freestyle flavor architecture for variety without SKU explosion.
Outcome / ROI framing

A coherent product stack from chemistry (clear protein) through robotics and subscription rails.

What shipped

Concept architecture, concentrate formulation notes, flavor systems, pod narrative.
