Case study · business narrative

Problem

Interactive 3D demos often look great in a recording and stutter on a mid-range laptop. Physics, materials, and React re-renders fight for the same frame.

Approach

  • React Three Fiber for scene graph composition with declarative React state at the edges.
  • Rapier for rigid-body physics decoupled from render when possible.
  • Instancing and asset reuse to cut draw calls; custom shaders only where they earn cost.
  • Profile-driven iteration: measure frame time, then cut work.
Outcome / ROI framing

A reactive spatial experience that holds interactive frame rates under load — reference for browser GL + careful state design.

What shipped

Simulator showcase and performance notes tied to broader graphics research.
